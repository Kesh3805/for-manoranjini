## 💌 About This Gift

A handcrafted, procedural 3D cinematic journey built entirely with **WebGL 2** and custom **GLSL shaders**. 

Through billions of light years and two universes, a lonely hero particle voyages across cold, starlit voids and cosmic nebulae—eventually discovering a warm, glowing ocean world where stardust gathers, blooms into flowers, spells her name in constellations, and forms a beating heart among the stars. 🌸🪐💫

## 🎮 Interactive Controls

| Key / Action | What it does |
|:---:|:---|
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | ⏸️ Pause / ▶️ Resume the journey |
| <kbd>→</kbd> | ⏩ Leap forward 10 seconds |
| <kbd>←</kbd> | ⏪ Rewind 10 seconds |
| <kbd>R</kbd> | 🔄 Restart from the very beginning |
| <kbd>F</kbd> / `Double Click` | 🖥️ Toggle Fullscreen mode |

> **Tip:** Open in fullscreen in a dark room with headphones on for the full cinematic experience! 🌙
